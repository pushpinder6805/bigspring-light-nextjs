faqs:
- title: Will updates also be free?
  answer: Updates and ongoing maintenance should be agreed as part of your project scope. Contact us to discuss what support your product needs and how you would like to plan future improvements.

- title: Discounts for students and Non Profit Organizations?
  answer: Tell us about your organization, goals, and budget. We can discuss a suitable scope together; any discount or special arrangement will need to be confirmed before work begins.

- title: I need something unique, Can you make it?
  answer: Yes. We develop websites, web applications, custom software, mobile applications, games, and integrations for SaaS platforms. Share your idea so we can explore requirements and the right technical approach.

- title: Is there any documentation and support?
  answer: Documentation, handover, and ongoing support can be discussed when defining your project. Let us know who will maintain the product and what help your team needs after launch.

- title: Any refunds?
  answer: Payment, cancellation, and refund terms depend on the agreement for your project. Please ask us to clarify those terms before starting work or making a payment.

- title: What is a product key?
  answer: A product key is a code used to activate licensed software. Custom development projects do not necessarily need one; licensing and access requirements can be discussed for your particular product.
- title: Can you improve an existing website or application?
  answer: Yes. We can review an existing product, improve its interface and performance, and work on integrations or new features. Start by sharing the current product and the challenges you want to solve.

- title: How do you estimate a project timeline?
  answer: Timelines depend on the scope, complexity, integrations, and feedback process. We first clarify the requirements, then discuss milestones and delivery expectations with you.

- title: Can you connect our existing SaaS tools?
  answer: We build custom integrations to connect platforms and automate workflows. The approach depends on the APIs, permissions, and data available from your chosen tools.
