A mobile form competes with a small screen and an on-screen keyboard. Thoughtful input behavior can turn a frustrating onboarding flow into a quick, understandable task.

## A practical approach

1. Choose the appropriate keyboard type, autofill hints, and input actions. Keep labels visible after typing and distinguish optional fields clearly.
2. Scroll focused fields and error messages into view without jumping unexpectedly. Let users move between inputs and dismiss the keyboard when needed.
3. Preserve entries during temporary network failures. Show submission progress, prevent accidental duplicates, and announce success or errors accessibly.

## Watch for this

A fixed footer button can become hidden behind the keyboard; test the complete form on real devices and larger text settings.

## Before you ship

Test the complete journey on Android and iOS with larger text, denied permissions, and an interrupted connection. Confirm that the app gives users a useful next step.
